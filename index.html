A little about me

Creative instinct.
Practical craft.

I’m a video editor and AI creator based in Odesa, Ukraine. I turn scripts and raw footage into concise, watchable stories — from AI-generated scenes and dialogue-free dramas to UGC ads, Shorts and longer compilations.

At VIRUM Production, I created Shorts from scripts and helped teammates build practical skills with AI tools and editing workflows. At Fabiosa Media, I edited source footage, created UGC creatives and adapted Reels for social platforms.

Some Shorts I worked on passed 100 million views; one edited Reel also crossed 100 million views.
